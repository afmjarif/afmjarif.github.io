I am pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at **Manarat International University (MIU)**, with a current CGPA of 3.61. My interests are cybersecurity and ethical hacking, embedded systems and robotics, and practical software development. I enjoy turning ideas into working systems, from Arduino-based robots to web applications used at university events.

Alongside my studies, I serve as **General Secretary** of the MIU CSE Club and as a **Research Executive** at the Institute for Advanced Research, MIU. I qualified for the final round of ICPC 2025, competed in the Line Following Robot(LFR) Contest at the Ismail Al-Jazari Robotics Fest 2025, and hold certifications including Claude 101 (Anthropic Academy) and Cyber Hero (AWS / Arena Web Security).
