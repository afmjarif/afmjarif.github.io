This site is where I keep my projects, events, certificates and notes in one place. I started it to document what I learn along the way: competitive programming for ICPC, building Arduino robots, experimenting with web projects, and exploring cybersecurity.

## What you'll find here

- **Events:** contests, fests and workshops I take part in.
- **Blog:** short notes on what I learn, mistakes included.
